## 转译方法论（国风主题 → 中性英文物质名词）

1. **文化标签禁用词**：Chinese / ancient / traditional / oriental / ink painting / ink wash / calligraphy —— 全部用英文物质名词直接描述对象
2. **元素 ≤3、分清主辅**：1 主体 + 1-2 辅助；特效/动作部件转静态视觉（雾气→mist puffs、光柱→beam of golden light、小船随水流→rowboat on a patch of blue water）
3. **一体两读句必写**：`reading as both [建筑类型] and [主题物]` —— v3 模板灵魂
4. **色彩 1 主 + 1 强调 + 1 点缀**，主色避开高饱和纯绿（防抠图误伤）
5. **固定层一字不改**（视角/盲盒比例/细节抑制/轮廓/风格/绿底/用途）
6. **辅助元素永远弱化词**：tiny / miniature / delicate / drifting / like a small toy
7. **有字牌匾一律 `no readable text`** 防乱码字

## 试点 Prompt（全文，其余 20 条见 JSON）

### penglai-isle（蓬莱 A 档）

```text
a single 3D isometric building, 45-degree top-down view,
a floating sky island crowned with a multi-tier pavilion palace with soft white mist drifting around its base,
the massive levitating rock island with layered jade-green tiled roofs and warm gold trim rises in bold stacked tiers, a soft beam of golden light glowing from its summit,
tiny white cloud puffs wrap delicately around the island base like miniature ornaments,
the entire structure reading as both floating island and celestial palace in one bold unified form,
blind box toy proportions big chunky block forms minimal fine details cartoon toy texture,
bold readable silhouette,
Rise of Kingdoms semi-realistic 3D rendered style, clean polished rendering,
jade green and warm gold with soft white mist accents,
centered filling the frame, pure green #00FF00 solid background,
game asset for 2D isometric mobile SLG world map city skin
```

设计点：空岛=主体（70%+），亭台楼阁=层叠琉璃顶概括化，雾气=微型云团绕底座，光柱=塔顶金色光束。

### canal-water-town（江南水乡 C 档）

```text
a single 3D isometric building, 45-degree top-down view,
a waterside pavilion house with a tiny wooden rowboat drifting beside it,
white plaster walls and deep blue tiled roofs stack in bold layers over a curved wooden hull base forming the dominant silhouette,
a tiny curved wooden rowboat floats delicately on a small patch of blue water at the base like a miniature toy,
the entire structure reading as both riverside pavilion and floating boat in one bold unified form,
blind box toy proportions big chunky block forms minimal fine details cartoon toy texture,
bold readable silhouette,
Rise of Kingdoms semi-realistic 3D rendered style, clean polished rendering,
white plaster and deep blue roof tiles with warm amber lantern accents,
centered filling the frame, pure green #00FF00 solid background,
game asset for 2D isometric mobile SLG world map city skin
```

设计点：粉墙黛瓦=`white plaster / deep blue tiled roofs`；小船=底座旁的微型木船；一体两读=「既是水榭又是船」（船形底座）；水流以底座一圈蓝色水面呈现，与绿幕色相区隔，抠图安全。

## 评审要点（每张图 8 项）

1. 45° 等距视角正确（建筑三面可见）
2. 盲盒玩具比例（大块面、无细碎、cartoon toy texture 生效）
3. 主体占 70%+ 视觉面积，辅助微小不抢镜
4. 一体两读成立（如蓬莱=既是空岛又是仙宫）
5. 无国风水墨偏差（是 RoK 半写实 3D，不是平面山水/水墨卷轴）
6. 纯绿背景干净、主体居中不贴边
7. 轮廓一眼可辨（缩小到图标尺寸仍可读）
8. 无乱码文字
